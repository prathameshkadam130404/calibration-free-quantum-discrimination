## Papers
1. Schlichtholz, Linowski, Walschaers, Treps, Rudnicki, Sorelli, "Practical tests for sub-Rayleigh source discriminations with imperfect demultiplexers", arXiv 2303.02654 [abstract + full text*]. One-vs-two equal-brightness, HG0-type null test with mode crosstalk (eps^2, p0~eps^2 background in the signal port under H0). Without crosstalk xi_Q = x^2 (x=our a0). With crosstalk: xi ~ {1 - [ln ln q - 1]/ln q} x^2, q = x^2/p0, for x >> eps; xi ~ x^4/(8 p0) for x << eps. Direct imaging xi_DI ~ x^4. Centroid fixed at origin; misalignment named but modelled only via generic crosstalk; no dark counts/estimated centroid.
   Verdict: point 1 PARTLY (same exponent-vs-background logarithmic law, but background from fixed mode mixing, not from centroid-estimation error; no F(kappa), no calibration photons). Mechanism: YES (this is it, the 1 - lnln q/ln q law). Point 2: NO.
2. Lu, Krovi, Nair, Guha, Shapiro, npj QI 4, 14 (2018), arXiv 1609.03025 / 1802.02300 [abstract + full text*]. Chernoff exponent for 1 vs 2 sources; B-SPADE/SLIVER optimal. Assumes perfect alignment; notes imperfect alignment can degrade; suggests using part of the light / direct imaging to estimate centroid before aligning; notes dark counts may hurt, suggests feedback. No exponent for estimated centroid (qualitative remark only). Verdict: point 1 NO (only qualitative remark), point 2 PARTLY (idea of splitting photons for centroid is mentioned, not optimised), mechanism NO.
3. Grace & Guha, PRL 129, 180502 (2022), arXiv 2107.00673 [abstract + full text* of first 100k chars]. Multi-hypothesis object discrimination; assumes centroids coincide at a location "known to the receiver" (prior or preliminary measurement). Claims TriSPADE keeps quadratic advantage under misalignment (e.g. theta/10 in a figure) and that crosstalk etc. "only reduce" accuracy; not quantified. Verdict: point 1 NO (misalignment shown only as small-offset robustness, no exponent theory), point 2 NO, mechanism NO.
4. Grace, Dutton, Ashok, Guha, JOSA A 37, 1288 (2020), arXiv 1908.01996 [abstract + full text*]. ESTIMATION of separation with unknown centroid; two-stage: direct detection (alpha T) -> centroid -> B-SPADE; ML with centroid as nuisance; adaptive alpha minimising estimator variance; residual centroid variance sigma_P^2=(sigma^2/n1)(1+theta^2/4sigma^2). Explicitly not hypothesis testing. Verdict: point 2 PARTLY (adaptive time split between direct imaging and sorter is known, for MSE not error exponent); point 1 NO; mechanism NO.
5. Zanforlin, Lupo, Connolly, Kok, Buller, Huang, Nat. Commun. 13, 5373 (2022), arXiv 2202.09406 [abstract only/search snippet; PDF unreadable]. Hypothesis test with two-mode interferometer; says SPADE is sensitive to misalignment as motivation. No misalignment/centroid analysis found (not verified in full text). Verdict: NO (unverified).
6. Huang & Lupo, PRL 127, 130502 (2021), arXiv 2106.00488 [abstract + full text*]. Exoplanet detection; assumes pointing at optical centre of mass; pointing error, dark counts, crosstalk deferred to future work. Verdict: NO.
7. Triggiani & Lupo, arXiv 2509.03511 (2025) [abstract/secondary only]. Chernoff exponent for arbitrary intensities; SPADE optimal only under covariance-commutation conditions. Review 2605.10767 says rotating receiver to principal axis without prior knowledge is open. Verdict: NO on S17, but relevant to "unknown frame" open problem.
8. Kariya, "Singular Asymptotics of SPADE in Quantum Source Discrimination", arXiv 2605.14432 (v3 Aug 2026) [abstract only; PDF unreadable]. One vs two under ideal alignment and fixed offset (misaligned); singular learning theory (Bayes free energy, RLCT); fixed offset removes SPADE's exact support mismatch (the null-test zero-background property) and gives same (1/2,2) exponents as direct imaging. Offset appears as fixed nuisance, not photon-estimated. Verdict: point 1 PARTLY (qualitative: misalignment destroys the exact-null advantage; Bayesian not Chernoff, no F value), mechanism PARTLY, point 2 NO. Should be cited/read in full before claiming novelty.
9. Choi, Baac, Jacob, Chung, arXiv 2607.06931 (v2 withdrawn) [abstract only]. Adaptive basis update, estimation + BIC model selection; tolerates misalignment up to 6 pixels in simulation. Not exponent theory. Verdict: NO (and withdrawn).
10. Related (not read): Grace et al. adaptive programmable sorter without prior knowledge (ResearchGate listing); Performance-tradeoff relation for locating two incoherent point sources, arXiv 2202.05647 / PRA 104, 022410 (snippet only: two-stage centroid then sorter, estimation).

## B. Log recovery of near-zero-background counting exponent
Found in Schlichtholz et al. (item 1) specifically for this problem. General Poisson/photon-counting: Wyner, "Capacity and error exponent for the direct detection photon channel" I/II (IEEE-IT 1988) and Wang-Wornell "refined analysis of Poisson channel in high photon-efficiency regime" (arXiv 1401.5767; log(1/E) - loglog(1/E) form, zero-dark-current case) are the nearest classical analogues [search snippets only; not verified that they state the same lnln/ln law]. Classical source is plausible but unverified.

Not found anywhere. Closest: Grace 2020 (variance of centroid estimate vs n1, estimation task).

## Overall verdicts
- S17 point 1 (universal separation-independent fraction F(kappa) of a0^2 for classical measure-first learners, incl. kappa curve): NOT FOUND. Components known: misalignment hurts null test (Kariya, Schlichtholz), centroid-from-direct-imaging idea (Lu 2018, Grace 2020).
- S17 point 2 (optimal splitting f=0.41, zero for kappa>=1): PARTLY KNOWN as concept (Grace 2020 adaptive alpha, estimation MSE; Lu 2018 remark); the exponent-optimal split NOT FOUND.
- Mechanism (exponent of near-zero-background test recovers only as ~1 - lnln q/ln q): KNOWN in this exact setting for crosstalk-induced background (Schlichtholz et al. 2023, Eq. 5). Novelty is only that background arises from centroid error and is tied to photon counts. Cite it; do not claim the log law as new.
Caveats: digests, not verbatim reads; 2202.09406 and 2605.14432 not read in full; Kariya v3 is most at risk of overlapping.
