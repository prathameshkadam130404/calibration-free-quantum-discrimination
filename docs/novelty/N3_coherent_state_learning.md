## 1410.8700 Sentis-Guta-Adesso [full text]
Task: discriminate |0> vs |alpha>, alpha unknown, localised Gaussian prior around KNOWN alpha0 (width ~1/sqrt n, "local" model), n training copies |alpha>^n, equal priors; n copies concentrated into |sqrt(n) alpha> by a beam-splitter chain (used only as a bookkeeping unitary). Figure of merit: excess risk R = n(P_e - P*_e) (1/n coefficient, finite function of |alpha0|). Compared: optimal collective measurement (R_opt, Eq 20) vs E&D with generalised (squeezed) heterodyne = optimal Gaussian estimation, then discrimination (R_E&D, Eq 35/39). Result: R_E&D > R_opt for all intermediate alpha0 -- "reaching more than a factor two at some points"; gap closes for alpha0->infinity (classical) and alpha0->0. Ratio is thus a CONSTANT in n (both ~1/n) that depends on alpha0; n-> infinity ratio is not 1. Conjectured (strong evidence, simplified model) that no local/non-Gaussian estimator closes the gap. Mechanism: no dark-port/interference explanation given; only generic "collective measurement". No numerical ratio values like 1.184 stated in text read.
Verdict: overlaps S16 YES for the generic fact (constant >1 asymptotic gap, coherent-state templates, joint vs Gaussian measure-first). Differences: their H0 is vacuum (known centre) with localised prior around known alpha0, vs S16 N(0,tau^2) pointing, H1 equal binary of known separation centred at theta (displacement-covariant, both hypotheses depend on theta); ratio independent of separation in S16.
## 1004.2468 Guta-Kotlowski [full text]
Qubits, local minimax, excess risk ~1/n. Optimal = estimate Helstrom measurement directly vs plug-in (estimate states + Helstrom). Quote: "the ratio between the optimal and state estimation plug-in risk is a constant factor". Gap strict except parallel Bloch vectors; Gaussian-model explanation (commuting Q1+Q2,P1-P2 vs non-commuting Q1-Q2,P1-P2 needs heterodyne). They say difference "would probably become significant in more interesting infinite dimensional models". Note: this is plug-in WORSE than optimal (opposite of S16's finding that plug-in is within 0.8% of Bayes, but S16's plug-in is with the Helstrom-optimal estimator for a different task; no contradiction but note).
Verdict: PARTLY (constant-ratio structure for qubits; plug-in not near-optimal there).
## 1208.0663 Sentis-Calsamiglia-Munoz-Tapia-Bagan [full text]
Qubits (pure, programmable discrimination). Optimal learning machine uses measure-first (training set measured, classical memory only, no quantum memory) and attains the absolute programmable-discrimination optimum: R_LM = R_opt = 1/(3n); E&D (estimate states, then discriminate) R = 2/(3n), "twice" the optimal. So measure-first with a discrimination-tailored measurement is optimal for qubits; estimate-then-discriminate is a factor 2 worse.
Verdict: PARTLY -- confirms measure-first can be optimal for qubits (so qubit case differs from S16/coherent where joint beats Gaussian measure-first); constant factor 2 gap for E&D.
